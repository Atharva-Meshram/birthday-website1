Happiest birthday, Sisooo! 🥹❤️🎀

Wishing you a very, very happy birthday, my Gulab ka phool. 🌷🤍 You are genuinely one of the sweetest people on Earth, and I hope you know just how important you are to me. And honestly, even though I don't like this month much, I love this date because of you. I keep waiting for your birthday, and I was SO excited about it even before today. 😭🎀 I kept thinking, “GUYS, IT'S GOING TO BE SISOOO'S BIRTHDAY!” and now it's finally here, and I get to wish my girl her special day. AAAAA, HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MY GULAB KA PHOOL! 🫶🏻💗

You know, meeting you was so random. I never imagined that someone I met so casually would eventually become such an important part of my life. It all happened so unexpectedly, but somehow, it was so needed. And now, you've become a part of my life that I never want to lose, someone who holds such a special place in my heart. If someone ever asks me why I believe in female friendships, I think my answer will always be you. Because you showed me how beautiful it is to have someone you can be yourself with, someone you can go days without talking to and still come back to with the same energy, the same vibes, the same stupid smiles and blushing over the most random things. 😭🎀 The way we enjoy talking to each other, the way the smallest interactions can make us so happy, and the way we can turn the most ordinary conversations into something memorable — all of it is so precious to me, just like you are.

And honestly, one of my favourite things in this world is seeing you happy. Even if it's something tiny that I can do to make you smile, it makes me happier than you could imagine. I don't think you realise how much happiness your happiness brings me. 🥹❤️

Sometimes, I think about our journey, and it genuinely makes me smile. From talking normally on Telly and having random interactions, to me being awkward for absolutely no reason, to slowly getting closer, playing Uno and Ludo, playing silly games, sharing the most random things and the silliest shit, and eventually being able to call each other, talk for ages, share our problems, and actually be there for one another. LIKE, LOOK AT US NOW. 😭🫂 Who would've thought we'd come this far? I certainly didn't, but I'm so, so glad we did. Every little turn our friendship has taken has brought us here, and I wouldn't trade these moments for anything. It's such a pretty little journey, and I'm genuinely grateful that it happened with you. 🤍

And now that you know just how important you are to me, there's something else I want you to remember, okay?

I know you're strong, and I agree with you. Of course, you're the baddie, Sisooo. THE BADDIE, INDEED. 💅🏻🎀 But listen to me, madam, you cannot hide everything behind that strong-girl attitude in front of me, okay? 😭 You can act all tough in front of the world, but you don't always have to do that with me.

Whenever something bothers you, whenever life feels too overwhelming, whenever you're confused, tired, hurt, or simply don't know what to do, I want you to know that you can always come to me. ANYTIME. ANYWHERE. You don't have to figure everything out on your own before talking to me. You don't have to wait until you're okay to come and talk to me. And you don't have to put your feelings into perfect words for me to understand that something is wrong.

Even if I can't fix everything, I'll always try my best to hear you out, understand you, help you however I can, and make things feel a little less heavy. And if you don't feel like talking, that's okay, too. You can simply sit there with me, say absolutely nothing, and still know that you're not alone. I want to be someone you can find comfort in, someone you can rush to when the world feels a little too hard on you, someone who reminds you that you don't have to carry everything by yourself. 🫂❤️

And no, I don't want to be a part of your life only when you're happy, cheerful, laughing, joking around, and everything is going perfectly. Of course, I LOVE those moments with you, but I want to be there for the other moments, too. When you're struggling, when you doubt yourself, when you're scared of what comes next, when you don't know where to go or whom to talk to. I want you to know that you have someone who genuinely cares about you, someone who will listen without making you feel like your feelings are a burden.

So, my Gulab ka phool, please never feel like you have to pretend to be okay with me. You deserve to be cared for even on the days when you don't feel your best. And I hope that, in whatever little ways I can, I get to make you feel as loved and cared for as you make the people around you feel. And if you ever find yourself forgetting how loved you are, I hope I get enough chances to remind you. 🥹🤍

Today, I want you to put all the stress, worries, and bad thoughts aside for a little while and just enjoy yourself. Eat good food, spend time with your favourite people, click the prettiest pictures, dress up, feel pretty, smile a little extra, and PLEASE DON'T FORGET TO SEND ME THE FIT CHECK. I REPEAT, THE FIT CHECK IS MANDATORY. 😭🎀

And if, at any point, you feel like it's just another normal birthday, remember one thing: IT'S MY GULAB KA PHOOL'S BIRTHDAY, OKAY? 🌹 So you better bloom like the prettiest rose in the garden, shine like the baddie you are, and let yourself enjoy every little bit of your day. Because today is about celebrating YOU — the person you are, the person you're becoming, and all the beautiful things that are yet to come into your life.
